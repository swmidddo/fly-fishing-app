Orvis "Rule of Three" Tippet Formula:

To find your starting tippet size, divide your fly hook size by 3. For example, a #12 hook works with 4X tippet ($12 \div 3 = 4$). Use Nylon Monofilament for high-buoyancy dry flies and Fluorocarbon for fast-sinking, invisible nymph & streamer presentations.

Uni-Knot

Line to Hook
Universal Tippet to Fly Hook Connection
Strength: 95%

The standard Uni-Knot is one of the most reliable terminal knots in all of angling. Extremely dependable, easy to tie in low light or cold weather, and holds tight on both monofilament and fluorocarbon lines.

📷 Step-by-Step Visual Diagram Aid
Step-by-Step Tying Instructions:
  1. Run 6 inches of tippet line through the eye of the fly hook.
  2. Lay the tag end back alongside the standing line to form a loop over both lines.
  3. Wrap the tag end around both lines 4 to 5 times, passing through the loop on each turn.
  4. Moisten line with saliva and pull the tag end to draw the wraps into a neat, tight barrel knot.
  5. Slide the barrel knot down firmly against the hook eye to lock the knot tightly.
  6. Trim tag end clean.
🎣

Improved Clinch Knot

Line to Hook
Tippet to Fly Hook (Universal Classic)
Strength: 95%

The most widely used terminal knot in fly fishing. Reliable, easy to tie, and holds tight on standard wire hooks (#8 to #18).

📷 Step-by-Step Visual Diagram Aid
Step-by-Step Tying Instructions:
  1. 1. Thread 3-4 inches of tippet through the fly hook eye.
  2. 2. Wrap the tag end around the standing line 5 to 7 times.
  3. 3. Thread the tag end through the small loop next to the hook eye.
  4. 4. Pass the tag end back through the large loop you just created.
  5. 5. Moisten with saliva and pull standing line steadily to tighten wraps neatly against the hook eye.
  6. 6. Trim tag end close.
➰

Non-Slip Loop Knot

Line to Hook
Tippet to Fly (Maximum Swimming Action)
Strength: 95%

The gold standard knot for tying streamer and nymph flies. Creates an open loop so flies swim with natural movement.

📷 Step-by-Step Visual Diagram Aid
Step-by-Step Tying Instructions:
  1. 1. Tie an overhand knot in the tippet line about 2-3 inches from the end. Leave it loose.
  2. 2. Pass the tag end through the eye of the fly hook.
  3. 3. Pass the tag end back through the loop of the overhand knot.
  4. 4. Wrap the tag end 4 to 5 times around the standing line above the overhand knot.
  5. 5. Bring the tag end back down and thread it through the overhand knot loop in the same direction it entered.
  6. 6. Moisten with saliva, pull the standing line and fly firmly to seat the knot tight, then trim the tag end.
